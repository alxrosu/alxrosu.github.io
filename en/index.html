01 / MY PERSPECTIVE

I love talking
about everything. And then some.

A story from work. A Romanian tradition. A day out in Zaragoza. My content starts with the things that happen to us and ends with a question that invites us to join in.

My storytelling brings together warmth, humour and a point of view. The editing follows the story: expressive typography, handwritten details, rhythm and emphasis on the moments worth remembering.

a good story carries something of you.
